About this app

Math Monster helps elementary school kids master addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division facts quickly and effectively. The app focuses on timed exercises and math drills that ensure children can recall each math fact confidently in just a few seconds.

This is the ultimate math fact fluency program designed for students in grades K-3 and up.

🚀 Why Parents & Teachers Choose Math Monster:

  • Complete Fact Coverage Master all four core operations: addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division. Build automaticity for success in future math homework and tests.
  • Targeted Fluency The app focuses on timed exercises that reinforce memorization and retrieval speed, essential for mental math skills.
  • Adaptive Learning Math Monster uses personalized practice that adapts to each child’s level, reinforcing weak facts until they are fully mastered.
  • Times Table Mastery Dedicated sections for mastering times tables up to 12. Ideal for 3rd grade and 4th grade students needing speed practice.

✨ Simple, Safe, and Effective:

Simple, clear, and distraction-free, Math Monster keeps children focused on improving their math skills. It’s the best way to practice math facts!

  • No Sign-Up or Accounts No personal information is required. Your child can start practicing immediately and safely.
  • Trackable Progress Monitor speed and accuracy daily to celebrate mastery and build a strong foundation in core math skills.
